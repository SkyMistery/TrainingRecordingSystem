## 2. What you agree to do

1. **Purpose.** Use the app only to prepare, run and debrief IVAO training and
   exam sessions.
2. **Consent before recording.** Record a session only after the trainee and
   every other participant in the voice call have agreed to be recorded
   (screen and voice). IVAO Rule 2.1.12 forbids recording private
   conversations without the consent of the participants. The app asks you to
   confirm this for every session and keeps the confirmation in the session.
3. **Say that you are recording.** IVAO Rule 2.1.12 requires users who record
   to note it as a remark in their flight plan or ATIS.
4. **No publication.** Do not publish, upload, post or distribute the
   recordings (video and audio), voice notes or transcriptions, in whole or in
   part — not on video platforms, social media, forums or chat servers.
   During the debriefing, share the review window live only with the trainee
   and, if needed, with IVAO training staff involved in their training.
5. **Screenshots.** The screenshots folder (`…_screen`) is meant for the
   training record: share it only with the trainee and with the IVAO training
   staff involved in their training, never publicly. Screenshots can show
   other users' callsigns, VIDs or names.
6. **Your notes are private.** Voice notes and transcriptions are your own
   assessment of the trainee. Keep them off shared screens (the app helps: see
   the User guide) and do not share them unless the trainee or IVAO training
   staff need them.
7. **Keep it safe, keep it short.** Keep the session folders on a computer and
   storage only you control, and delete them when you no longer need them for
   the training. How long you keep them is your decision; follow the law and
   any rule of your IVAO division.
8. **Respect people's rights.** If someone you recorded asks to see, or to
   delete, what you recorded about them, handle the request as the law
   requires.
9. **Follow the law.** Comply with the laws that apply to you and to the
   people you record, and with the IVAO Rules and Regulations.

If you can't do these things, don't use the app.
